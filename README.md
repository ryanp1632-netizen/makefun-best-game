**MARGINALIA: The Hare Knight's Last Folio** is an original action game set on the page of an illuminated
manuscript. Ink blots, armoured snails, spitting gargoyles and bookworms crawl out of the margins; you move the
Hare Knight with WASD and *draw with the mouse*. Wet ink burns foes and walls them in, and a stroke that closes
into a loop **Illuminates** every foe inside it in a burst of gold leaf. The more you catch in one loop, and the
faster you chain loops (the Flourish, up to x5), the more you score.

- **Goal:** save the manuscript across three folios (The Garden Margin, The Bestiary, The Last Leaf), three
  verses each, each folio ending in a Grotesque boss: Sir Gastropod (crack his shell with a loop), the Bookwyrm
  and the Great Blot.
- **Win:** blot all three Grotesques. **Lose:** the hare's hearts run out.
- **Depth:** 6 foe kinds and 3 bosses with two phases, 15 rubrics (upgrades, picked 1 of 3 at every level up),
  wax seal (SPACE) and hop (SHIFT) cooldowns, 3 difficulties, grades S-D, 8 feats, best scores and recent runs.
- **Made from scratch:** every sprite, the parchment, the music (procedural church modes) and every sound
  (Web Audio synthesis) are drawn or generated by the game's own code. No libraries, no assets, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```

Controls: WASD / arrows move, hold the left mouse button to draw ink, SPACE or right click stamps the seal,
SHIFT hops, 1-3 pick a rubric, ESC pauses, R restarts, M mutes.

Hand-in program: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts` copies `game/` and `screenshots/` and
writes `dossier.json`. Screenshots are real renders made by `tools/shots.sh` (headless Chrome) from the scenes in
`game/src/shots.js`, which play the actual game forward with its autopilot. `node tools/simtest.mjs 5 scribe`
plays whole runs headlessly to check the balance.

## Code map

- `game/src/sim/`: the rules (run, player, ink and loops, foes, bosses, director, weapons, pickups, rubrics,
  scoring, autopilot); no DOM, so it also runs in Node.
- `game/src/art/`: parchment, hare, foes, bosses, ink, effects, icons and the scene painter.
- `game/src/ui/`: HUD, title page, rubric pick, pause and results screens.
- `game/src/audio/`: synthesised sound effects and the procedural score.
